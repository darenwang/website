About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of ACMS at the University of Notre Dame. Previously, I was a Postdoc at the University of Chicago under the supervision of Prof. Rebecca Willett. Before that, I completed my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University under the supervision of Prof. Alessandro Rinaldo and Prof. Christopher Genovese.

For Prospective Students

I am looking for new Ph.D. students to join my group. Our group includes members from diverse backgrounds such as applied mathematics, computational physics, machine learning, and statistics.

Research Interests

  • Scientific computing in high dimensions
  • High-dimensional linear algebra and its applications to machine learning
  • Nonparametric estimation in high dimensions
  • Change point detection and inference
